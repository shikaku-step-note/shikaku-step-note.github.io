# IT Passport Ver.6.5 technology depth register

Source: [IPA detailed syllabus Ver.6.5](https://www.ipa.go.jp/shiken/syllabus/omgdg50000005kn1-att/syllabus_ip_ver6_5.pdf). Page numbers are the printed numbers. The register maps the added examples and assessment evidence and does not assign complete status to every individual glossary term.

## ip-binary — 33 (p. 35)

`固定されたビット幅で計算する`: signed range and overflow. `条件を変更すると、どの行が増えるか`: logical search conditions.

New assessed practice: ip-binary-depth-1, ip-binary-depth-2, ip-binary-depth-3.

## ip-statistics — 34 (pp. 35–36)

`抽出のしかたから確率の式を決める`, `代表値と分布を別々に読む`, `グラフと制約を使って判断する`. Adds finite sampling, scales, variability, directed graphs and queue reasoning. Additional worked instruction: `推定・検定・回帰が答える問いを分ける` and `変化率と合計を単位で確認する`. Further exam-like applied exercises for these subskills remain to be added.

New assessed practice: ip-statistics-depth-1, ip-statistics-depth-2, ip-statistics-depth-3.

## ip-ai-data — 35 (pp. 36–37)

`連続した測定値をビット列にする`, `分類の成績を見逃しと誤警報に分ける`, `生成結果と参照元を照合する`. Applies sampling, classifier evaluation and source verification; `文字の対応表と、推論の前提を確認する` adds encoding and inference examples.

New assessed practice: ip-ai-data-depth-1, ip-ai-data-depth-2, ip-ai-data-depth-3.

## ip-algorithms — 36–37 (pp. 38–39)

`反復が終わったときの変数の意味を決める`, `データが整列しているときだけ探索範囲を半分にする`. Adds boundary tracing, binary search prerequisites and FIFO/LIFO. Adds sorting/merge traces and tree structures in `整列と併合を途中状態まで追う` and `木にしたときの親子と番号の違いを読む`.

New assessed practice: ip-algorithms-depth-1, ip-algorithms-depth-2, ip-algorithms-depth-3.

## ip-languages — 38–39 (p. 39)

`表示・意味・動作をそれぞれ直す`, `変換の成功と実行結果を分ける`. Applies language roles, XML structure and error timing.

New assessed practice: ip-languages-depth-1, ip-languages-depth-2, ip-languages-depth-3.

## ip-computer — 40–42 (pp. 40–41)

`処理が止まる場所をCPU・メモリ・入出力から探す`, `転送速度と端末の性能を切り分ける`. Adds bottleneck analysis, memory role and transfer calculations.

New assessed practice: ip-computer-depth-1, ip-computer-depth-2.

## ip-architecture — 43–44 (pp. 42–43)

`直列と冗長を、停止条件から見分ける`, `速さ・処理量・復旧を別々に測る`. Covers mixed configurations, common-cause caveat, throughput/latency and MTBF/MTTR.

New assessed practice: ip-architecture-depth-1, ip-architecture-depth-2.

## ip-os-files — 45–46 (pp. 44–45)

`同じファイル名でも到達先が違う理由`, `利用者の権限と資源の不足を切り分ける`. Adds relative-path trace, permissions and virtual-memory impact.

New assessed practice: ip-os-files-depth-1, ip-os-files-depth-2.

## ip-office-oss — 47–48 (pp. 45–46)

`コピーした式がどのセルを参照するか予測する`, `ソフトウェアを配る条件まで調べる`. Adds reference traces and license-use decision.

New assessed practice: ip-office-oss-depth-1, ip-office-oss-depth-2.

## ip-hardware — 49 (p. 47)

`機器の形状、信号、役割を順に確認する`, `どの向きに情報が動くかを見る`. Uses troubleshooting and data-flow direction to distinguish device roles. The original generated WebP under `作業用PCに接続された機器を見分ける` illustrates USB input, external storage and the LAN connection.

New assessed practice: ip-hardware-depth-1, ip-hardware-depth-2.

## ip-design — 50–51 (pp. 48–49)

`申込画面を、入力して直せる順に設計する`, `同じ情報でも、探したい目的で並べ方を変える`. Applies form elements, error recovery, LATCH and human-centred evaluation.

New assessed practice: ip-design-depth-1, ip-design-depth-2.

## ip-media — 52–53 (pp. 50–51)

`データ量を「1回分 × 回数」で求める`, `復元の必要性から形式と圧縮を選ぶ`. Adds raw media sizing and format trade-offs.

New assessed practice: ip-media-depth-1, ip-media-depth-2.

## ip-database — 54–55 (p. 52)

`注文の行が何を一意に表すか決める`, `対応関係を保存する表を決める`. Adds composite keys, referential links and normalization/point-in-time trade-offs.

New assessed practice: ip-database-depth-1, ip-database-depth-2.

## ip-sql-transactions — 56–57 (pp. 53–54)

`行の絞込みとグループの絞込みを区別する`, `一緒に終えるべき処理をひとまとまりにする`. Applies filtering/grouping and atomic transactions/competing updates.

New assessed practice: ip-sql-transactions-depth-1, ip-sql-transactions-depth-2.

## ip-network — 58 (pp. 54–55)

`端末から外のネットワークまでを区切って調べる`, `回線の速度と待ちを分けて見積もる`. Adds layer/role diagnosis, throughput/latency calculation and IoT/edge trade-offs in `IoTの回線を距離・電力・量から選ぶ`.

New assessed practice: ip-network-depth-1, ip-network-depth-2.

## ip-protocols — 59–60 (pp. 55–56)

`ブラウザでページを開くまでの通信を追う`, `送るものと優先する性質から方式を選ぶ`. Applies protocol roles and basic isolation of connectivity, naming and rights.

New assessed practice: ip-protocols-depth-1, ip-protocols-depth-2.

## ip-security-risk — 61–62 (pp. 57–58)

`資産への影響から優先順位を付ける`, `対応を選び、残るリスクを引き受ける人を決める`. Applies CIA, risk ownership, residual impact and management review.

New assessed practice: ip-security-risk-depth-1, ip-security-risk-depth-2.

## ip-security-controls — 63 (pp. 59–62)

`予防・検知・復旧を別々に組み立てる`, `確認したい性質に対策を結び付ける`. Defensive-only coverage of authentication/authorization, layered safeguards and recovery objectives.

New assessed practice: ip-security-controls-depth-1, ip-security-controls-depth-2.

## ip-cryptography — 63 (pp. 59–62)

`書類を誰の鍵で扱うか整理する`, `検証できる内容と証明書の範囲を確かめる`. Applies key ownership and trust scope to document delivery.

New assessed practice: ip-cryptography-depth-1, ip-cryptography-depth-2.

Additional independent checks for ip-statistics: ip-statistics-depth-4, ip-statistics-depth-5, ip-statistics-depth-6.

Additional independent checks for ip-algorithms: ip-algorithms-depth-4, ip-algorithms-depth-5.

## Item 35.4–35.5 deeper review (IPA Ver.6.5 pp. 36–37)

The new model-choice, error propagation, model architecture, and transfer-learning sections explicitly teach feature bias and generalization, nonlinear activations, loss/gradient roles, CNN/RNN/GAN, foundation models, and changes to model weights versus changes to inference inputs. Worked examples and assessment cover selection and interpretation. Matrix algebra and implementation-level training are not claimed by the basic-concepts objective.

New assessed practice: ip-ai-data-depth-4, ip-ai-data-depth-5, ip-ai-data-depth-6, ip-ai-data-depth-7, ip-ai-data-depth-8, ip-ai-data-depth-9.

## Item 58.2 deeper review (IPA Ver.6.5 p. 54)

Worked choices now distinguish physical and logical networks, switch/MAC and gateway roles, inter-segment permissions, connection placement and weak mesh backhaul. Both link/troubleshooting decisions and IoT trade-offs have fresh exercises.

New assessed practice: ip-network-depth-4, ip-network-depth-5, ip-network-depth-6.

## Items 59.1–59.2, 60.2–60.3 deeper review (IPA Ver.6.5 pp. 55–56)

Adds a seven-layer/four-layer interpretation case, role of ports, NTP, FTP and email functions. Communications case compares handover and roaming, MVNO and ISP, SIM/eSIM/MNP and usage-based charges. Graded questions require layer/protocol selection and original fee calculation.

New assessed practice: ip-protocols-depth-3, ip-protocols-depth-4, ip-protocols-depth-5, ip-protocols-depth-6.

## Item 34.1–34.2 assessment cross-check (IPA Ver.6.5 pp. 35–36)

The syllabus describes basic concepts and lists hypothesis testing as an example. It does not prescribe a particular hypothesis test, reference distribution or test-statistic calculation in the item. The lesson now teaches interpretation and an optional exact, simplified one-/two-sided counting case with stated null, independence, no ties, probability calculation, decision and limits. Graded checks cover new conditions, original data, standard deviation, deviation score and measurement uncertainty. The explicit queue-model exclusion belongs to item 34.4 only. No broader categorical exam exclusion is inferred from the absence of a prescribed test formula.

New assessed practice: ip-statistics-depth-7, ip-statistics-depth-8, ip-statistics-depth-9, ip-statistics-depth-10.
