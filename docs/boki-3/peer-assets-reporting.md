## Quality and boundaries

Other worked conditions remain inside the current 2026 grade3 scope. Disposal/declining-balance and formal bank-reconciliation/advanced accounting are not introduced as current required skills. Corporate tax percentages and VAT simplifications are expressly example conditions, not current real-company tax rates or individualized advice. Input conditions specify whether current depreciation is already booked or must be included in one combined sale entry; the models follow that choice. Compound entries, dividend decisions versus payments, reserves as internal equity transfers, prior-tax payments versus current expense and VAT gross-versus-net treatment were reviewed substantively.

Japanese explanations generally teach the reason and sequence, with intermediate calculations and changed conditions. Repeated accounting names are useful technical precision. The author is completing language-tool edits separately; this peer review checked natural meaning and content, not merely a style score.

The standalone worksheet and reporting banks contain row/field drills as well as full worked tables. To claim complete independent table-production practice, the release must also include the root's fresh capstones/full mock grids. Do not equate the ability to answer a few detached balance fields with constructing an entire integrated table. Course-wide evidence and mock review remain separate release checks.

Snapshot hashes in the witness file identify the reviewed bank versions. Later changes to questions, models, scoring or the full tables require appropriate recheck. No expert endorsement, examination equivalence or pass prediction is established by this review.
